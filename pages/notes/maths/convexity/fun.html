Convex function

Overview

A function whose epigraph is a convex set. Epigraph is the portion above a curve. This function takes in a vector and outputs a scalar similar to an objective function. $$ f(x) \; : \; \mathbf{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbf{R} $$

If the hessian (second derivative, determines the curvature) of a curve is strictly positive, the curve is a convex function. QPs that have this property are said to be strongly convex. Parts of curve-2 in the figure curve down, i.e. has negative curvature/hessian and hence isn't convex.

Examples

Linear

$$ f(x) = c^\top x $$

Quadratic

$$ f(x) = \frac{1}{2} x^\top Qx + q^\top x, \quad Q \geq 0 $$ If $Q$ is positive semi-definite, the curve has saddle points and is said to be weakly convex.

Norms

All norms (1-, 2- etc.) are convex. $$ f(x) = \|x\| $$
