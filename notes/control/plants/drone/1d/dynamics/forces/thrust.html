Thrust

Each spinning rotor pushes air downwards. By Newton's third law, the air pushes the rotor (and the vehicle) upwards with an equal and opposite force called thrust. The force that actually acts on the drone points upwards. Direction of thrust is always perpendicular to the plane that the rotor spins in. $$\begin{align*} F_t &\propto \omega^2 \\ &= k_f \,\omega^2 \\ \end{align*}$$ The constant of proportionality, $k_f$ is the thrust coefficient. Refer lift constants. When the rotation speed of a rotor is doubled, thrust increases by a factor of 4.
