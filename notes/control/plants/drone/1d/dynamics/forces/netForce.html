Net force

Overview

A monorotor has only one rotor and therefore only one thrust vector pointing upwards in the positive $z$ direction. This will cause an acceleration in the z direction. $$ F_z = m|g| - |F_t| $$ $F_z$ is the net force on the vehicle.

Linear translational acceleration

$$ \ddot{z} = g - \frac{F_t}{m} $$

Translational equilibrium

When a monorotor is hovering, it is in translational equilibrium.

To hover ($F_z=0$), the thrust force upwards would need to be equal to the force that gravity exerts downwards. $$\ddot{z} = 0$$
