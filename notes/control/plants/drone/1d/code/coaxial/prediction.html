State prediction

State transition function

$$\begin{align*} g(\hat{x}_{k-1|k-1}, u_{k-1}, \Delta{t}) &= \begin{bmatrix} \dot{z}_{k-1|k-1} + u_{k-1} \, \Delta{t} \\ z_{k-1|k-1} + \dot{z}_{k-1|k-1} \, \Delta{t} + \frac{1}{2}u_{k-1} \,\Delta{t}^2 \end{bmatrix} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ \Delta{t} & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \dot{z}_{k-1|k-1} \\ z_{k-1|k-1} \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \Delta{t} \\ \frac{1}{2} \,\Delta{t}^2 \end{bmatrix} [u_{k-1}] \\ &= A_{k-1}\,x_{k-1|k-1} + B_{k-1}\,u_{k-1} \end{align*}$$
$$\begin{array}{ccl} A &:& \text{System matrix} \\ B &:& \text{Input matrix} \\ u_{k-1} &:& \text{Control input to the system in the previous time step} \\ \end{array}$$

Jacobian of the State transition function

$$\begin{align*} G_t &= g'(x_{k-1|k-1}, u_{k-1}, \Delta{t})\\ &= A_{k-1} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ \Delta{t} & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{align*}$$

Prediction step

$$\begin{aligned} \hat{x}_{k|k-1} &= g(\hat{x}_{k-1|k-1}, u_{k-1}, \Delta{t}) \\ \Sigma_{k|k-1} &= G \Sigma_{k-1|k-1} G^T + W \end{aligned}$$
