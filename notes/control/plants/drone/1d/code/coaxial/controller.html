Overview

$z$ is used to denote the innovation. But, here it represents the altitude.

Error terms: $$\begin{align*} e &= z_d - \hat{z} \\ \dot{e} &= \dot{z}_d - \dot{z} \\ e_i &= e_i + (e \cdot \Delta{t}) \end{align*}$$ PID terms: $$\begin{align*} P &= k_p \cdot e \\ D &= k_d \cdot \dot{e} \\ I &= k_i \cdot e_i \end{align*}$$ New torque demand: $$ u = P + I + D + \ddot{z}_d $$
