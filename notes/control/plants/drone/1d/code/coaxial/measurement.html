Measurement

Random gaussian noise is added to the true state, to get the measured state. Only the altitude is measured. $$\begin{align*} y_{z_k} &= x_{z_k} + v_k \\ v_k &\sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) \\ \sigma &= 0.01 \end{align*}$$

Measurement model

$$\begin{align*} h_k &= C_k\hat{x}_{k|k-1} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \dot{z} \\ z\end{bmatrix}_{k|k-1} \\ H_k &= h' = C_k \end{align*}$$

Updates

Innovation $$\begin{align*} z_k &= y_k - h_k \\ S_k &= H_k \Sigma_{k|k-1} H_k^T + V \end{align*}$$ Kalman gain $$ L_k = \Sigma_{k|k-1} H^\top S_k^{-1} $$ State $$\begin{align*} \hat{x}_{k|k} &= \hat{x}_{k|k-1} + L_k \, z_k \\ \Sigma_{k|k} &= (I - L_kH_k) \Sigma_{k|k-1} (I - L_kC)^\top + L_k V L_k^\top \end{align*}$$
