Setup

Aim

The aim is to control a coaxial drone using measurements obtained by an altitude sensor.

The drone's position and velocity are $z = 0$ and $\dot{z} = 1$ initially. It is commanded to change its position by 1 meter in the negative (up) direction, i.e. its desired altitude is $z=-1$.

State

If yaw is ignored, the state variables are: $$ x = \begin{bmatrix}\dot{z} \\ z \end{bmatrix} $$

Initialisation

$$\begin{align*} \hat{x}_{0|0} &= \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0\end{bmatrix} \\ \Sigma_{0|0} &= \begin{bmatrix}\sigma_{\dot{z}}^2 & 0 \\ 0 & \sigma_{z}^2 \end{bmatrix} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix}0.1^2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.1^2 \end{bmatrix} \\ \end{align*}$$

Estimation initialisation

$$\begin{align*} W &= \begin{bmatrix}\sigma_{\dot{z}}^2 & 0 \\ 0 & \sigma_{z}^2 \end{bmatrix} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix}0.1^2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.1^2 \end{bmatrix} \\\\ V &= \sigma_{v}^2 = 0.1^2 \\\\ \Delta t &= 1 \end{align*}$$

Control input

A 1D drone's acceleration, its only control variable, influences its vertical velocity and its height, $z$. $$u = [\ddot{z}]$$

Initialisation

$$ u = 0 $$
