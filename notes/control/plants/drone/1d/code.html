To simplify things, assume direct control over acceleration. $u$ is used to represent a thrust or torque control input to the drone. For a 1-D monorotor, the equation of motion is: $$ \ddot{z} = g - \frac{u}{m} $$ $u$ is the upward thrust that can be controlled. To show it has a direct influence on the body-acceleration, the dynamics equation can be written in a simpler form: $$ \ddot{z} = \bar{u} $$ This simpler form is a reminder that the quantity being controlled is the body-acceleration. Velocity and position is then the integral of acceleration and velocity respectively.
