Error

Consider that the aim is for a monorotor to hover: $$\ddot{z} = 0$$ The target position is a constant altitude since the aim is to hover.

Initial state: $z(0) = \dot{z}(0) = 0$

Assume the drone's mass has been overestimated by a small amount, i.e. the drone is lighter than the controller thinks. The controller will apply more torque to the rotors and hence more thrust to the drone than is necessary.

Consider the drone in the hovering state. Due to the mass mismatch, there would be a small constant error in the drone's linear acceleration. With a small error in mass, the position error will grow quadratically with time.

Refer effect of errors in double integrators.
