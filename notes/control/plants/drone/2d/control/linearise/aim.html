Aim

The aim is to decouple the following non-linear equations by linearising them. $$\begin{align*} \ddot{\phi} &= \frac{u_2}{I_{xx}} \\ \ddot{y} &= \frac{u_1}{m}sin\phi \\ \ddot{z} &= g - \frac{u_1}{m}cos\phi \end{align*}$$
$$\begin{array}{ccl} u_2 = M_x = (F_1 - F_2)\cdot Lcos\theta &:& \text{Rotational moment about x} \\ u_1 = F_t = F_1 + F_2 &:& \text{Thrust in the } -z_b \text{ direction, upwards.} \\ \phi &:& \text{Roll about x, making the angle w.r.t. } z_w \\ \theta &:& \text{Fixed angle between the drone's arms and the body frame.} \end{array}$$
$u_1, u_2$ are the parameters that can be controlled.
