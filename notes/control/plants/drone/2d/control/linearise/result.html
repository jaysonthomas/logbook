Linearised non-linear equations

Linearised equations around the hover state: $$\begin{align*} \ddot{y} &= g\phi \\ \ddot{z} &= g - \frac{u_1}{m} \\ \ddot{\phi} &= \frac{u_2}{I_{xx}} \end{align*}$$

What is being controlled?

Controlling $z$

By setting $u_1$, the drone's vertical acceleration can be controlled directly. As time passes, this influences the drone's vertical velocity which then influences its vertical position. A PID controller can be used to control such second-order double-integrator systems. $$ u_1 \xrightarrow{\text{sets}} \ddot{z} \xrightarrow{\int} \dot{z} \xrightarrow{\int} z $$

Controlling $y$

The aim is to control it as a second-order double-integrator system as above. The ideal solution would be: $$ ?? \xrightarrow{\text{sets}} \ddot{y} \xrightarrow{\int} \dot{y} \xrightarrow{\int} y $$ From equation 1, $\phi$ needs to be changed to set $\ddot{y}$. But, $\phi$ can only be controlled via its second derivative which can only be controlled via $u_2$: $$ u_2 \xrightarrow{\text{sets}} \ddot{\phi} \xrightarrow{\int} \dot{\phi} \xrightarrow{\int} \phi \xrightarrow{\text{sets}} \ddot{y} \xrightarrow{\int} \dot{y} \xrightarrow{\int} y $$ A small error in $\ddot\phi$ grows quartically with time when computing $y$. $$ e_y(t) \propto t^4 $$ Refer effect of errors in double integrators.
