What zero accelerations mean

At the equilibrium point (hover), the second derivatives are all 0, yielding: $$\begin{align*} \frac{u_2}{I_{xx}} &= 0 \\ \frac{u_1}{m}sin\phi &= 0 \\ g - \frac{u_1}{m}cos\phi &= 0 \end{align*}$$ From the 1st equation: $$ u_2 = 0 $$ Squaring and adding equations 2 and 3: $$\begin{align*} \frac{u_1^2}{m^2} (sin^2\phi + cos^2\phi) &= g^2 \\ u_1 &= mg \end{align*}$$ Thus, substituting in equation 3: $$\begin{align*} \frac{mg}{m}cos\phi &= g \\ cos\phi &= 1 \\ \phi &= 0 \end{align*}$$ To achieve zero accelerations, the moment and roll needs to be 0 and the thrust needs to match the drone's weight.
