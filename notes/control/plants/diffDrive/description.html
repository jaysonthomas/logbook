Links & joints

base_footprint

This is the World Origin. This frame sits flat on the ground where $Z = 0$.

base_link

base_link is the robot chassis.
  • Shape & Size: It is a 3D rectangular box measuring 0.6 meters long (X-axis), 0.4 meters wide (Y-axis), and 0.2 meters high (Z-axis).

  • Appearance: It is colored blue.

  • Positioning: The visual origin is offset vertically by 0.1 meters (base_height / 2.0). This means the physical bottom face of the chassis sits exactly at the origin of the base_link coordinate frame, rather than the coordinate frame being stuck dead-center inside the box.
Before applying any offset, the origin sits exactly at the geometric center of the box. By applying the vertical offset, the entire box is shifted upward so that its bottom face lines up perfectly with the origin ($Z = 0$) of the base_link frame.

base_right_wheel_joint

base_right_wheel_joint is the right wheel axle serving as the rotational actuator connecting the main chassis (base_link) to the right wheel (right_wheel_link).

How its configuration translates to the physical world:

  • Joint Type (continuous): It allows for infinite, unrestricted rotation.

  • Axis of Rotation: The wheel spins around the pitch axis (the Y-axis).

  • Physical Location: The joint positions the right wheel at a very specific spot relative to the center of the chassis:
    • X = -0.15 meters (-base_length / 4.0): It is shifted toward the rear of the robot.
    • Y = -0.225 meters (-(base_width + wheel_length) / 2.0): It is shifted to the right side, pushing the wheel just outside the main body frame so it doesn't scrape against the blue chassis.
    • Z = 0: It sits exactly at the base level of the chassis frame.
