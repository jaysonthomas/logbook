Work through

Setup

Consider a symmetric gaussian distribution representing a car's position, centered perfectly at a mean of $\mu = 0$ with a variance of $\sigma^2 = 2$.

Also, consider a simple nonlinear dynamics function: $$g(x) = x^2$$

Comparison

The standard KF would simply pass the original mean/state through the function to get a new estimate: $$g(\mu) = 0^2 = 0$$

But, because the function is $x^2$, any point on the left side of the original distribution (e.g., $x = -2$) gets squared and becomes positive ($+4$). Any point on the right side (e.g., $x = 2$) also becomes positive ($+4$). Every single piece of uncertainty gets thrown into the positive domain. The true expected value is actually $2$. The actual centre of mass of the system has moved to 2.
