Non-linear transformation of a gaussian

It is possible to pass the mean and variance of a prior distribution through a non linear transformation to get a new mean and variance. It is possible to assume a gaussian distribution around the new mean and variance. However, the shape of the true distribution underneath would be warped.

When a perfect circle or ellipse of uncertainty (a Gaussian) is passed through a highly nonlinear function - like an S-shaped curve, a sine wave, or polar-to-Cartesian coordinate switching - the distribution gets stretched, squashed, and bent. The true resulting distribution might look like a banana, a crescent, or a multi-modal blob. Such a distribution cannot be perfectly described by just a mean and a covariance anymore.
