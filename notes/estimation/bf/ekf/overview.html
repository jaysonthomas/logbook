Overview

A gaussian distribution gets warped when passed through a non-linear system. Because the standard Kalman filter cannot handle non-gaussian shaped distributions, EKF forces a system to be linear.

Here, the beliefs are gaussian but the state or observation models are non-linear.

EKF calculates a tangent line (a Jacobian) at the centre mean point (the current estimated state). It then pretends the non-linear system/curved function is just that flat, straight line. Gaussian distributions get passed through this linearised system to output gaussians again. Everything then behaves just like a KF.

Linear approximations have to be made at every time step. EKF is hard to implement because even the smallest amount of error throws the estimator.
