Measurement model

Definition

A measurement model takes a state as input and returns the measurement expected to be seen from a perfect sensor for that state. $$\begin{align*} h_k &= h(\hat{x}_{k|k-1}) \\ &= C\hat{x}_{k|k-1} \end{align*}$$
$$\begin{array}{ccl} C &:& \text{Measurement/Output matrix} \\ \end{array}$$

Jacobian of the measurement model

It aims to answer the question, how sensitive is the measurement prediction to errors in the current-state prediction $\hat{x}_{k|k-1}$. $$ H_k = h'(\hat{x}_{k|k-1}) = C_k $$ For a linear measurement model, the Jacobian is just $C$.
