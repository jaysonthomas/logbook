so(3)

Overview

This is the space of angular velocities.Elements: $3 \times 3$ skew-symmetric matrices, $[\omega]$.Structure: Unrolled from a 3-element angular velocity vector $\omega = [\omega_x, \omega_y, \omega_z]^T$:$$[\omega] = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\omega_z & \omega_y \\ \omega_z & 0 & -\omega_x \\ -\omega_y & \omega_x & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$Physical Meaning: Instantaneous rotational velocity. Also called the Special Orthogonal Group. This is the group which represents continuous geometric spaces of 3D rotations. It represents all possible orientations a rigid body can have in three-dimensional space, centered at an origin.

Elements

$3 \times 3$ rotation matrices, $R$.

Constraints

To be a valid rotation, the matrix must satisfy $R^T R = I$ (orthogonal) and $\det(R) = 1$ (special, meaning no reflections/mirroring).

Degrees of Freedom (DoF)

3 (roll, pitch, yaw).
