Euler's equation

[Ref]

General form of Euler's equation in a rotating frame: $$ M = I\dot\omega + \omega \times (I\omega) $$

Euler's rotation theorem

It states that any 3D rotation or sequence of rotations can be described as a single rotation by an angle $\theta$ around a single 3D unit vector axis $\mathbf{u} = (x, y, z)$.
