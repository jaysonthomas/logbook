Determinant

Overview

[Ref] The determinant gives an idea of how much an area (in 2D and volume in 3D) is being squished, scaled or flipped.

Determinant of 1

$$\lvert{R}\rvert = 1$$ Because the absolute value is $1$, the transformation does not grow, shrink, or squish space. If the sign is negative, the transformation has turned space inside out. A right hand has been transformed into a left hand. This only happens if a reflection (a mirror flip) has occurred.

For a matrix to represent a pure rotation, it must satisfy two conditions:

  • It can't stretch things out (distances between points must stay the same).
  • It can't turn things inside out (an object in 3D space cannot be rotated and suddenly become its own mirror image).
