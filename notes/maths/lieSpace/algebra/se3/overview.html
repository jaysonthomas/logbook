Overview

The Lie algebra of rigid transformations is the space of spatial twists (combined linear and angular velocities).

Elements

$4 \times 4$ matrices, $[\mathcal{V}]$.

Structure

Unrolled from a 6-element twist vector $\mathcal{V} = (\omega, v)$, where $\omega \in \mathbb{R}^3$ is angular velocity and $v \in \mathbb{R}^3$ is linear velocity: $$ [\mathcal{V}] = \begin{bmatrix} [\omega] & v \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Physical Meaning

The instantaneous screw motion (twist) of a rigid body. This representation is used to represent a $6 \times 1$ twist matrix as a $4 \times 4$ matrix.
