Cross products

Cross product of $\mathbb{R}^6$ vectors

The Lie bracket of $V_1$ and $V_2$: $$\begin{align*} V_1 \times V_2 & = [ad_{V_1}]V_2 \in R^6 \\ [ad_V] &= \begin{bmatrix} [\omega] & 0 \\ [v] & [\omega] \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{6 \times 6} \end{align*}$$

Matrix bracket notation of cross product of $\mathbb{R}^{4 \times 4}$ matrices

Matrix form of lie bracket: $$ [V_1 \times V_2] = [V_1][V_2] - [V_2][V_1] \in se(3) $$
