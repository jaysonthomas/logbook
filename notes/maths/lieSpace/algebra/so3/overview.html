Overview

This is the space of angular velocities.

Elements

$3 \times 3$ skew-symmetric matrices, $[\omega]$. $$[x] = -[x]^\top$$

Structure

Unrolled from a 3-element angular velocity vector $\omega = [\omega_x, \omega_y, \omega_z]^\top$: $$ [\omega] = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\omega_z & \omega_y \\ \omega_z & 0 & -\omega_x \\ -\omega_y & \omega_x & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Physical Meaning

Instantaneous rotational velocity.
