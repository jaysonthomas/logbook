SE(3)

Overview

Also called Special Euclidean group. It represents continuous geometric spaces of 3D rigid-body transformations. It combines both rotation and translation to describe a full 6 DoF pose in space.

Elements

$4 \times 4$ homogeneous transformation matrices, $T$.

Structure

It has a block structure containing a rotation matrix $R \in SO(3)$ and a translation vector $p \in \mathbb{R}^3$ $$ T = \begin{bmatrix} R & p \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} $$

Degrees of Freedom (DoF)

6 (3 for rotation, 3 for translation).
