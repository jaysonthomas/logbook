SO(3)

Overview

Also called the Special Orthogonal Group. This is the group which represents continuous geometric spaces of 3D rotations. It represents all possible orientations a rigid body can have in three-dimensional space, centered at an origin.

Elements

$3 \times 3$ rotation matrices, $R$.

Constraints

To be a valid rotation, the matrix must satisfy $R^T R = I$ (orthogonal) and $\det(R) = 1$ (special, meaning no reflections/mirroring).

Degrees of Freedom (DoF)

3 (roll, pitch, yaw).
