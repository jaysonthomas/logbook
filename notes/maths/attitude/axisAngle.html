Axis angle

Overview

$$ \phi = a \, \theta $$
$$\begin{array}{ccl} a &:& \text{Axis of rotation, a unit vector.} \\ \theta &:& \text{Angle of rotation, a scalar in radians.} \end{array}$$
$\phi$, called axis angle, is a vector $\in \mathbb{R}^3$.

Properties

$$\begin{align*} \|\phi\| &= \theta \\ \frac{\phi}{\|\phi\|} &= a \end{align*}$$
$$\begin{array}{ccl} \|\phi\| &:& \text{Norm of the axis angle} \\ \frac{\phi}{\|\phi\|} &:& \text{Normalisation of the vector} \end{array}$$
The magnitude of an axis angle is the angle of rotation. The direction is the axis of rotation.

Gyro usecase

For a constant $\omega$ from a gyro, with short sample periods $h$ (say at 100Hz), the integral of $\omega$ gives $\phi$: $$ \phi \approx \int^h_0 w(t)\, dt $$ This is only true for short time scales and small angles.
