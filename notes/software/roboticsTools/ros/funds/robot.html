Robot

The body of a robot consists of two components
  • Links: The rigid pieces
  • Joints: The pieces that move, enabling motion between connected links

Joints

A wheel joint is a revolute joint, connecting a wheel link to a base link. A revolute joint enables rotational motion. Fixed joints have no motion. It could be a simple screw that connects a LIDAR to a robot's base. Prismatic joints cause linear motion between links.
