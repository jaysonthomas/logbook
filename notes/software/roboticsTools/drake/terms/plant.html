Plant

Overview

MultibodyPlant contains the entire multibody world: every rigid/articulated body in the simulation, robots and objects alike, all living in one shared tree of bodies/joints/frames, with one shared SceneGraph for geometry.

Each model, added to a plant, gets its own ModelInstanceIndex, but they all share:

  • one continuous/discrete state vector for the whole plant
  • one SceneGraph for collision/visual geometry
  • one coordinate/frame tree, with cross-body relationships (e.g. contact between the iiwa's gripper and the brick) handled natively

Finalize locks the model. After this, no more bodies or joints or actuators can be added.Finalization computes internal data structures.

Use plant.GetModelInstanceByName("iiwa") for example, to get the ModelInstanceIndex.
