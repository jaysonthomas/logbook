LeafSystem

LeafSystem is the smallest building block in Drake used for processing inputs, maintaining state, and producing outputs. A leaf system could represent a single:
  • Physical component - spring, damper, sensor or a RigidBodyPlant
  • Mathematical component - functions such as Adder, Multiplier, and TrigExp
Leaf systems in Drake have well-defined inputs and outputs, which can be either:
  • Continuous signals: position or velocity
  • Discrete events: button presses or sensor readings

They are often implemented as C++ classes that inherit from the LeafSystem base class.
