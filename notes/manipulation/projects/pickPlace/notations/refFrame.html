Reference frame

A frame is represented with the xyz axes. XYZ => RGB => FMT. The right-hand rule (vehicle coordinate system) is used.
The aim is to get different reference frames to match; because once they do, the algebra becomes trivial. E.g., once reference frames match, positions can be added.

Digital representation

The numerical representation for a frame, say $O_{initial}$ (an object's initial frame), on disk is its pose - $^WX^{O_{initial}}$.

A frame cannot be represented - only a representation that is relative (pose) can be represented on disk.

A frame defines the semantics of how to connect 2 poses. A pose is the manifestation that holds the position and orientation of a frame relative to some other frame.
