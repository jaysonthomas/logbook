Make gripper frames

Aim

Given $X^{O_{\scriptsize{initial}}}$, $X^{O_{\scriptsize{goal}}}$ and $X^{G_{\scriptsize{initial}}}$, calculates intermediate trajectory keyframes and corresponding timestamps needed to execute the pick-and-place task.

It returns a pair of 2 maps linking a keyframe's name with the corresponding:

  • Pose
  • Timestamp when the gripper should reach the frame

Initial & final pick/place poses

$$\begin{align*} \newcommand{\pose}[4]{^{\scriptsize{#2}}\hspace{-#4em}#1^{\scriptsize{#3}}} \pose{X}{}{G_{pick}}{0} &= \pose{X}{}{O_{initial}}{0} \cdot \pose{X}{O}{G_{grasp}}{0.35} \\ \pose{X}{}{G_{prepick}}{0} &= \pose{X}{}{G_{pick}}{0} \cdot \pose{X}{G_{grasp}}{G_{pregrasp}}{0.4} \\ \pose{X}{}{G_{place}}{0} &= \pose{X}{}{O_{goal}}{0} \cdot \pose{X}{O}{G_{grasp}}{0.35} \\ \pose{X}{}{G_{preplace}}{0} &= \pose{X}{}{G_{place}}{0} \cdot \pose{X}{G_{grasp}}{G_{pregrasp}}{0.4} \\ \end{align*}$$

Clearance pose

$\pose{X}{G_{prepick}}{G_{clearance}}{0}$ is formed based on the angle and axis of rotation obtained from $\pose{X}{G_{prepick}}{G_{preplace}}{0}$. Refer Euler's rotation theorem. Half of the angle and translation distance is used. The clearance frame is placed 3 cms higher in the Y direction w.r.t. the prepick frame. $$\begin{align*} \pose{X}{G_{prepick}}{G_{preplace}}{0} &= (\pose{X}{}{G_{prepick}}{0})^{-1} \cdot \; \pose{X}{}{G_{preplace}}{0.4} \\ \pose{X}{}{G_{clearance}}{0} &= \pose{X}{}{G_{prepick}}{0} \cdot \pose{X}{G_{prepick}}{G_{clearance}}{0.4} \end{align*}$$
