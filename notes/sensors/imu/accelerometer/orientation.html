Equations: $$\begin{align*} tan \,\phi_{xyz} &= \frac{a_y}{a_z} \\ tan \,\theta_{xyz} &= \frac{-a_x}{a_y \, sin\,\phi + a_z \, cos\,\phi} \\ &= \frac{-a_x}{\sqrt{a_y^2 + a_z^2}} \\ \end{align*}$$
