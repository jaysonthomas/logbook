Optimisation problem

An optimization problem is usually written as $$\begin{aligned} \min_x \quad & f(x) \\ \textrm{s.t.} \quad & g(x)\leq 0,\\ \quad & h(x)=0 \end{aligned}$$ We call $x$ the **decision variable**, $f(x)$ the **cost function**, $g(x)\leq 0$ an **inequality constraint**, and $h(x)=0$ an **equality constraint**. We usually denote the optimal solution by $x^*$. Most of the times, the constraints are hard-constraints, meaning that they must be fulfilled by the optimal solution.
