Optimisation problem

An optimization problem is usually written as $$\begin{aligned} \min_x \quad & f(x) \\ \textrm{s.t.} \quad & g(x)\leq 0 \\ \quad & h(x)=0 \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{array}{ccl} x &:& \text{Decision variable} \\ f(x) &:& \text{Cost function} \\ g(x)\leq 0 &:& \text{Inequality constraint} \\ h(x)=0 &:& \text{Equality constraint} \end{array}$$
Most of the times, the constraints are hard-constraints, meaning that they must be fulfilled by the optimal solution.
