Constrained discrete optimisation problem

Constraints are enforced at each time step. They are usually evaluated for the knot points and not for the finer interpolated points due to the higher computational expense.

Following are different types of constraints:

Dynamics constraints

They are a set of equality constraints enforced at every time step. RK4 could be used to obtain the below for example. $$ s.t. \quad x_{k+1} = f(x_k, u_k) $$

Actuator constraints

These are scalar bounds on $u$, torque limits, which are inequality constraints. $$ u_{min} \leq u_k \leq u_{max} \quad \forall k $$

Collision avoidance constraints

These are obstacle avoidance or safety constraints. They are inequality constraints on the state. For e.g. distance from the minimum radius around an object needs to be above some threshold. $$ c(x_k) \geq 0 \quad \forall k $$
